WEEK 4 Professional Growth October 1, 2026

Learning to Communicate More Intentionally

One thing I have been thinking about this semester is how much effective communication depends on both the audience and the quality of the reasoning behind the message.

In my MCOM class, we have discussed different logical fallacies, including bandwagon reasoning and appeals to authority. Recognizing these has made me more aware of how easily an argument can sound convincing without actually being well supported.

Organization is also a major part of how I approach my work. I genuinely enjoy planning and consider it one of my stronger skills. I use several digital tools, but I still rely heavily on a physical planner to manage deadlines, meetings, priorities, and tasks.

WEEK 3 Academic Learning September 24, 2026

Connecting Art History, Business, and Ethics

One of my favorite parts of school is finding connections between subjects that initially seem unrelated. This semester, I have been able to combine my interest in art history with business ethics and professional communication.

For my MCOM class, I am researching a business article titled When Wealth Becomes a Moral Dilemma: Religious Patronage in Early Fourteenth-Century Italy.

The project is teaching me how to narrow a broad topic, evaluate credible academic sources, and connect ideas from art history, religion, economic history, and business.
